# The Winged Races & the Winged Wars - GM

## Design Rationale

- The Aarakocra/Harpy split is built on a single clean axis: *born to fly* vs *granted flight*, with the singing voice as the stick in the craw.

## Aarakocra (Player Race)
See D&D.

## Harpy (Player Race) - D&D5e
**Ability Score Increase** – your Dexterity score increases by 1 and your Charisma increases by 2.

**Age** – harpies are born from eggs and grow into flight-capable chicks in the nest after a couple of years. They then mature like most men, reaching adulthood at 15 years. Material Harpies tend to live for 85 years, like normal men. Harpies in the Fey are trapped in the symbolic cycles like all creatures there, and do not age. Harpies in the Shadow Realm have their existence stretched out over centuries, until they too eventually fade.

**Alignment** – Harpies are jealous and cowardly, the results of their unremembered lost war, and as such are self-centred and quick to judge. They are neutral, only really caring for themselves and their nests. Due to that neutrality it is not uncommon, however, to find Good or Evil harpies, though they tend to be more chaotic than lawful, as they have long since lost trust in Law.

**Size** – your size is Medium.

**Speed** – your base walking speed is 30ft.

**Winged Arms** – you have a flying speed of 50 feet. To use this speed, you can't be wearing medium or heavy armour, or wielding a weapon without the light property in either of your winged arms.

**Talons** – your talons are considered natural weapons, in which you are proficient, dealing 1d4 slashing damage on a hit. You can use your Dexterity modifier with your Talons as if the Talons had the *finesse* property.

**Subrace** – Harpies have three subraces, each which determines their *song*.

### Raven Harpy

**Song of Memories Lost** – As a Raven Harpy, you can use your action to sing the song of memories lost. A number of humanoids of your choice that you are aware of equal to your proficiency bonus within hearing (300ft) must make a wisdom saving throw against a DC of 8 + your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus. If they fail, they are *stunned* until the song ends as memories they had long forgotten come thundering back into the fore of their minds to consume all their attention. You must use your bonus action on subsequent turns to maintain the song, though you may end it at any moment. The song ends if you are incapacitated. A creature can repeat the saving throw if it is harmed, and at the end of each of its turns.

A creature that succeeds the save, initially or otherwise, is immune to your song for the next 24 hours. You cannot use spells with a verbal component whilst singing.

### Dream Herald

**Song of the Sublime** – As a Dream Herald, you can use your action to sing the song of the sublime. A number of humanoids of your choice that you are aware of equal to your proficiency bonus within hearing (300ft) must make a wisdom saving throw against a DC of 8 + your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus. If they fail, they are charmed by you and your allies until the song ends, regarding you and your allies as their close friends to be heeded and protected, though they will not take actions to harm themselves or loved ones. You must use your bonus action on subsequent turns to maintain the song, though you may end it at any moment. The song ends if you are incapacitated. A creature can repeat the saving throw if it is harmed, and at the end of each of its turns.

A creature that succeeds the save, initially or otherwise, is immune to your song for the next 24 hours. You cannot use spells with a verbal component whilst singing.

### Lost Harpy

**Trickery** – you are proficient in Deception.

**Mimicry** – you can perfectly mimic sounds you have heard, including voices. A creature that hears the sounds you make can tell they are imitations with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check opposed by your Charisma (Deception) check.
