# The Imperial Heartlands

Often called the Imperial Core or Heartlands, the territory West of the Fold has always held the main portion of developed and long-standing imperial land, ever since the formation of the first Empire from the Three Kingdoms all based there.

Geopolitically the region is historically split between the polities south of the Lynol bay (excluding Ainne, the youngest duchy) and those north, with Czondazan being a distinct but nearly always subjugated territory representing the entire north, though in recent years they’ve all started to be seen as just the “heartlands”, especially since the conquest of Stannis and the reformation of the 2nd Empire. These halves are known as the Northern and Southern Heartlands. 

Some still draw lines in the sand between the core of the old kingdom of Elóna, Czondazan and Damaran, and even the old “colonies” of Salsburry, Vabaria and Ainne, especially amongst ancient folk with long lives to whom all this was yet only a few generations ago.

### Geopolitics

**The Crownlands** - the central heart of Imperial power. See below.

**Czondazan** - the northern kingdom first subsumed in the birth of the Empire hundreds of years ago, now and for many centuries split between its three duchies of Poznan, Volhynia and Tchernigow, each named for the city at their heart. Czondazani people to this day are fiercely protective of their national spirit, and set themselves apart from imperial citizens. The laws of the Empire certainly do on many occasions.

**Salsburry** - the first frontier of Elóna, the once-called Southern March is now the Duchy of Salsburry, named after the first Imperial Duke to take up the title, Willem Salsburry, in 962 A.C. Its major settlements are Lye-On-Sea, Barlewyck, Willemwell, and Avonsby. The Duchy is a broad band stretching from the Wester Sea’s bulging Lynol Bay to the Upper Lineé. The Salsburry Heights line its south, a set of ridgelines, lake littered vales and small mountains.

Squeezed between imperial expansion and the very different peoples to the south in Damaran, they formed a fused culture of the two, and is a populous region and source of productive citizens and fertile soils for the demands of the Crownlands.

**Vabaria** - composed of three main regions, the Varian Highlands of the Spur, the Varian Plain to the southwest, and the Alta Terra Deorum (the "Altrador") plateau. The capital is Var, an ancient refuge city, due to its protective divine shell erected prior to the Cataclysm. It now sits at the commanding intersection at the rubbled tip of the old Spur mountains and the plains, keeping watch over the course of the rivers at its foot.

The Varian plains are sometimes seen as cursed: difficult to protect for all history, prone to flooding and mud, and frequent victim of unnatural damage (such as dragon attacks during the dragon war, the formation of the Fold crumbling the nearby mountain system on top of it, roving Legions of Terror even after the Cataclysm, tormented spirits seeping out of the rivers from the Godfall...). All the more reason protective Var is said to be one of the most densely populated cities in the Empire.

**Damaran-Olistia** - The large united kingdoms of Damaran-Olistia ("D-O") are famously formal allies of Elóna whose royal marriage unification brought about the 1st Empire. The kingdom of Olistia itself is a doughnut state with its “capital” of Hus being a quasi-independent city state of its own, the famous city of wooded spires. This makes D-O a union, of a union, of a union. The kingdoms cover the vast terrain between the escarpment defining Salsburry and the Viexes Corronas to the south-west, the entire Erynraef ("wood-lace") river basin including the upper section and technically covers the Hyarmendor Barca, the largest arid land (including stretches of dune covered desert) in the Heartlands. 

Damaran-Olistia is the only polity west of the fold whose crown is not directly held by Emperor Stannis. Nevertheless, they were loyal supporters of the formation of the Second Empire. They were a bastion of imperial civilisation in the south and anvil to Stannis’ New Army hammer; and arguably the main reason Stannis’ reconquering of the southern heartlands from Sorcerer Kings was so successful. The potential of a two-front war with the two greatest powers West of the Fold caused many to cede without much of a fight.

**Duchy of Ainne** - the south-eastern portion of the heartlands, the duchy is a frontier. Since the founding of the Empire and actual colonisation of distant lands, to the crusades post-deicide using Ainne's capital, Ainne the city, as its forward operating base to retake the outsider-blighted lands. Its plentiful natural resources and the great White Lake at its heart facilitating irrigation and transportation makes this a prosperous, if dangerous, place. Even after the Crusades of White pushed all the way into The Desolation, far to the south of Ainne city.

**Bailiwick of Coers** - a small old noble republic of majority halflings tucked into the Valleys of the Vine, encroaching only mildly into the Alta Terra Deorum and what inhabitable regions of the Valley of the Gales that has been reclaimed. They are well known for the maintenance of their Republic through the latter half of the Age of Strife after the defeat of The Galeforce Wizard; and their superb wine and olive production that brings disproportionate wealth to the otherwise minor region. After brief skirmishes and smooth diplomacy, the Bailiwick was incorporated as an autonomous region into the Second Empire, and provides its own Corps of troops for Stannis’ New Army, the Bailiwick Battalion - with its high proportion of well-outfitted halfling broom riders.

**Ludoro** - The fragmented polity that controls the lands beyond the Viexes Corronas. Well known for its dangerous often sea-inundated gold mines, mercenary companies, mercantile savviness and piratical naval prowess, including fearsome pirate Sorcerer Kings during the Age of Strife who never really went away, simply bending the knee to the deep pockets of the Empire. The lowering of sea levels post-deicide saw an almost unprecedented gold rush and booms of prospects and piracy across these shores.

#### The Glades
Spread across the heartlands, the glades form a loose federation of polities defined by their shared Wyld nature and loose alignment with the Empire.

- **Ghelron** (Czondazan) - largest and arguably least wild "wild" glade, surrounding the northern mountains and passes through and around. They're imperially aligned, and they hold a chokehold over land-trade with northern Czondazan.
- **Hus** (Olistia) - better known as the Wooded City of Spires, it is the heart of the D-O Kingdom and few refer to it as a glade at all, as it is the only true example of a Wild Elven city.
- **Vensi** (Ludoro) - The isles that make up the glade of Vensi, off the coast of Ludoro, are poulated by wild elves well-known ofr island-hopping and springing ambushes from the mangroves. They're popular privateers for the Empire. (Dare you say pirate!)
- **Maeryn** (north flank of the Spur) - high up in the northern foothills, the Maeryn are well known roamers whose mission seems to be gathering the flora of the shard and beyond, taking samples from everywhere. Their sparse but incredible diverse high-altitude gardens are a wonder of the Empire. Despite being far further, more Maeryn elves can be found in Guiezzia than Ghelron elves.
- **Gravewood** - in the crownlands is an area once known as the Alderwood, but for many generations a darkness and murikiness has grown from there. Now it is known as The Swars, or the Gravewood, and the glade that were there have gone silent.
- The Ebon Lady and Her **Ebon Glade** (Ainne) – a notable non-imperial citizen, the Ebon Lady is an ancient creature and her glade is only nominally imperially aligned, assisting in catastrophe's but infamous for hunting down errant woodsmen that stray too deep into the Ebon Glade or Mountroot Forest.

Smaller groups also exist, such as the wardens of the crimson wood in Czondazan, though no more formal glades are named by the Empire.

### Geography
Mitteland is a vast shard, and the largest known of the Great Materiae of the Home Constellation, until the discovery of Tlancayo, with a pre-Deicide population roughly on par with Patrie du Pape. Its geography and peoples are many and varied, especially as it is described through time.

**The Imperial Core / Heartlands** – the name of the western third of the Great Materia, stretching from Czondazan in the north to Ludoro and the blasted Desolation of southern Ainne in the South. The Fold is its eastern border and the Wester Sea its western one. The vast majority of the imperial population and power lie within it.

**The Fold** – the massive mountain range that divides the heartlands, roughly the western third of the shard, from the rest. Formed during the unimaginable violence of the Shattering, it dominates the shard and has shaped its history immeasurably. Stretching from north to south, petering out near the southern mist-wall, only the treacherous southern Mountroot forest or well-guarded Northern Pass allow over-land passage, with the Khazmod Deeps being the traditional passage under the mountains since the birth of the Empire.

**The Spur** – the remains of the old mountain chain that dominated this region of Eden. It crumbled into the Vabarian Highlands west of the fold, and forms the backbone dividing Ihleiska and the Mid-West until it collapses into the valleys of High Castle at the Rift.

**The Wester Sea** – often just called “the sea”, by far the largest body of water on the entire shard and plagued to its west by the ephemeral Mistwall churning the waters to the point one cannot tell mist from Mist, services the heartlands and its collection of rivers and coastal towns, providing a highway that has helped immeasurably in maintaining the cohesion of the Empire, its early growth, and in particular its heartland.

**Lynol Bay** – the bulged remains that are the Vabarian Highlands meets its opposite, the large Lynol Bay, with the effect of narrowing the heartlands (proportionally, the actual width still covers hundreds of miles). Whilst the bay itself is a rather typical patch of sea, this narrowing splits the heartlands into roughly northern and southern portions, and coincides with two of the three primary culture groups that make up the modern heartlands (Elonian and Damaran). Political unity for some 500 to 1000 years along with pollination from colonisation has seen these two cultures merge in many aspects, but artefacts of these cultures are still clearly visible today. (Czondazan is the third major group).

**The Drifting Isles** – an area of land oft locked in ice north of Czondazan, the islands here have a curious propensity to be replaced wholesale by semi-regular Mist surges. They are a Mistdiver’s wet dream, even if posing occasional and incredible danger to the region, and are completely unsettleable of course.

**The White Lake** – the largest lake and body of fresh water in the shard, it is the heart and oft-quoted source of the Linneus. It also forms the heart of the Duchy of Ainne. It is so named for its semi-frequent transformation into a white colour, as well as the “good” properties that the many spa towns/resorts on its borders, ever since the White Crusades to retake the blasted lands of the Godfall Craters.

**The Linneus / The Lineé** – the main river of the Empire that flows from the White Lake of Ainene up through the plains of Vabaria, tumbles down the falls of Steyncraw, winds through the green fields of Salsburry, and then generally winding westwards through the Crownlands. On its banks sits the Capital of the Empire, and its great estuary spans tens of miles as it empties into the Wester Sea at Guiezzia. The upper portion is called the Linneus, and the lower the Lineé.

**The Desolation & The Godfall Crater** – the site of the Godfall, surrounded by lesser tormented creatures of the outerplane stranded and wandering, and a harsh desert-like environment where life-force consistently fails to take hold. Huge chunks of Ainne and Damaran are lost to this blighted zone.

**Viexes Corronas** - a line of mountains far to the south-west that separate Ludoro from the rest of the Heartlands. Only half the height of the Fold, these peaks are ancient places even today littered with Edenic ruins and lost souls. A few mapped routes exist through it, but most take the journey north, or sail. South lies only great danger and death in many forms: La Muerte Verde and Los Acantilados Embruxados. Even if one were to traverse the southern Corronas, one would only find themselves deep in the dry and hostile environment of Hyarmendor Barca.

**The Swars** – the marsh that spreads deep across the land, running from the west sea many miles inland in the southern portion of the crownlands. It is a dangerous place of black mana and missing souls, and those that prey and thrive on such things. Folklore has it, the marsh grows. If one were to consult hundreds of years old maps it would seem so, though scholars would say simply that old maps are inaccurate.

### The Crownlands, The Capital & Imperial Governance
The Crownlands, primarily much of the old Kingdom of Elóna, stretch across much of the lower river basin of the Lineé (née Linneus). Indeed, it’s all the basin where the river is called the “Lineé”, stretching north and south to border Czondazan, the first Imperial conquest, and Salsberry, the old Imperial March, respectively. To the west it butts against the sea, only giving way to the free city of Guiezzia, that succeeded during the Age of Strife after a back-wash of colonial mercantile influence. To the southeast it shares a long border with the old duchy of Vabaria, and the east itself, all the way to the looming Fold, is known as the “uplands” and is primarily farming, logging and mining countryside with a few market towns and many, many yokels and halflings.

Lying on the wide and lazy Lineé in the heart of the Lineé basin, is the Capital. It is sometimes referred to by its old name of Yorlburgh(“/jɔ:brə/"), or erroneously by its former kingdom name of Elóna. While it’s also called the City of Three Hills, most simply refer to it as “The Capital”. Here the Bronze Throne, seat of the Emperor, lies in the Imperial Palace atop the tallest of the three hills, aptly named the “Bronze Hill”, surrounded by the House of the Diet and the estates of the governing families that make up the Imperial Diet as well as the domiciles and work places of its most prominent organs; and the home-from-homes of the Princes. The government itself is made of three departments; the Emperor, The Diet (composed of the bureaucratic class), and the Chamber of Princes that holds Imperial family as well as the various landed aristocrats that rule significant portions of the Empire, both on the home shard and abroad.

On the second hill, the “Ivory Hill”, sits the theological heart of the Empire. During the reign of Elóna and the First Empire, the various Gods in favour or vogue had their temples attended to, with the Panthodic Forum, the Parthenon, forming the administrative heart of the sprawling complex that dominated the top third of the hill. The Second Empire saw the ascendency of worship of the Everlight, the shining heart of Sendum and the ideology of superior First Man, as Stannis was a firm believer and supporter. The young Stannis nearly lost all support, almost causing rebellion in the streets of Capital itself, with what rioting that did occur being brutally beaten down, when the Parthenon was demolished, but he forged his New Army with zeal, tempered them in the river purges and reconquest of Czondazan, and along with shrewd statesmanship, including the encouragement of militia inquisition groups and adherence to the new state religion for any and all appointments in government and army, saw him rise as he did.

The First Empire’s Biblias Magikum, remodelled and under new ownership of the New Army as of the Second Empire as the Imperial College circa 1760A.C., sits astride the lowest of the three main hills, “Parchment Hill”. Taking over magical education and research, it also grew several new wings as part of its role in officer training, licence control and rogue mage hunting, introducing significant areas of administration and martial function. Due to this change in ownership the much-changed organisation that was the Biblias Magikum built a new academy of civilian arts across the river in the low-town circa 1776A.C. The New Academy of Civilian Arts, (“Naça”) is definitely not run by a vampire coven pair who gutted and remodelled the Biblias Magikum, profess Everlight adherence though have never been seen in the cathedral, and have worked long and hard on research into Time and the Soul. However, it is the one official issuer of mage licenses for non-army members, though licenses can be earned and collected at other official or vetted locations, such as the University in Willemwell, and they play the game of politics well enough that their influence and use is such that their predilections have never been called to light.

In the Second Empire, the New Army training grounds is located at the army township some miles downstream at the northern tip of a long bend of the Lineé, (un)creatively renamed “Training Grounds”. There, they’re well known for conducting wargames within the town and its many surrounding grounds, and the rocky hinterland of the southern Ghelron forests (though, of course, not within the grounds of the Glade itself), whose enormous northern woodland is a source of the renowned Mitteland lumber. 

The heartlands, as one might expect of a fertile and (relatively) stable locale with history, is littered with villages, towns and even cities. The greatest are Capital and Guiezzia, but strung between them and radiating outwards are over a dozen places rightfully called cities, and countless towns and villages throughout. Loebeck, Sallafell, Bendorf, Cumania (“Coomny”), Wulmsborough, and Yontervalend, just to name a few. 6 others are marked on the map [to be player defined].
