# The Imperial Heartlands

Often called the Imperial Core or Heartlands, the territory West of the Fold has always held the main portion of developed and long-standing imperial land, ever since the formation of the first Empire from the Three Kingdoms all based there.

Geopolitically the region is historically split between the polities south of the Lynol bay (excluding Ainne, the youngest duchy) and those north, with Czondazan being a distinct but nearly always subjugated territory representing the entire north, though in recent years they’ve all started to be seen as just the “heartlands”, especially since the conquest of Stannis and the reformation of the 2nd Empire. These halves are known as the Northern and Southern Heartlands. 

Some still draw lines in the sand between the core of the old kingdom of Elóna, Czondazan and Damaran, and even the old “colonies” of Salsburry, Vabaria and Ainne, especially amongst ancient folk with long lives to whom all this was yet only a few generations ago.

### Geography of Mitteland
Mitteland is a vast shard, and the largest known until the discovery of Tlancayo, with a population roughly on par with Patrie du Pape. It’s geography and peoples are many and varied, especially as it is described through time. The (relatively) unchanging features of the face of this land, however, are;

**The Imperial Core / Heartlands** – the name of the western quarter of the Great Materia, stretching from Czondazan in the north to Ludoro and the blasted Desolation of southern Ainne in the South. The Fold is its eastern border and the Wester Sea its western one. The vast majority of the imperial population and power lie within it.

**The Fold** – the massive mountain range that divides the heartlands, roughly the western third of the shard, from the rest. Formed during the unimaginable violence of the Shattering, it dominates the shard and has shaped its history immeasurably. Stretching from north to south, petering out near the southern mist-wall, only the treacherous southern Mountroot forest or well-guarded Northern Pass allow over-land passage, with the Khazmod Deeps being the traditional passage under the mountains since the birth of the Empire.

**The Spur** – the remains of the old mountain chain that dominated this region of Eden. It crumbled into the Vabarian Highlands west of the fold, and collapses into High Castle at the Rift.

**The Wester Sea** – often just called “the sea”, by far the largest body of water on the entire shard and plagued to its west by the ephemeral Mistwall churning the waters to the point one cannot tell mist from Mist, services the heartlands and its collection of rivers and coastal towns, providing a highway that has helped immeasurably in maintaining the cohesion of the Empire, its early growth, and in particular its heartland.

**Lynol Bay** – the bulged remains that are the Vabarian Highlands meets its opposite, the large Lynol Bay, with the effect of narrowing the heartlands (proportionally, the actual width still covers hundreds of miles). Whilst the bay itself is a rather typical patch of sea, this narrowing splits the heartlands into roughly northern and southern portions, and coincides with two of the three primary culture groups that make up the modern heartlands (Elonian and Damaran). Political unity for some 500 to 1000 years along with pollination from colonisation has seen these two cultures merge in many aspects, but artefacts of these cultures are still clearly visible today. (Czondazan is the third major group).

**The Drifting Isles** – an area of land oft locked in ice north of Czondazan, the islands here have a curious propensity to be replaced wholesale by semi-regular Mist surges. They are a Mistdiver’s wet dream, even if posing occasional and incredible danger to the region, and are completely unsettleable of course.

**The White Lake** – the largest lake and body of fresh water in the shard, it is the heart and oft-quoted source of the Linneus. It also forms the heart of the Duchy of Ainne. It is so named for its semi-frequent transformation into a white colour, as well as the “good” properties that the many spa towns/resorts on its borders, ever since the White Crusades to retake the blasted lands of the Godfall Craters.

**The Linneus** – the main river of the Empire that flows from the White Lake up through the border realm of Salsberry and the plains of Vabaria, generally westwards through the crownlands and winding westwards, and empties in the capital bay at Guiezzia.

**The Desolation & The Godfall Crater** – the site of the Godfall, surrounded by lesser creatures and a harsh desert-like environment where life-force consistently fails to take hold. Huge chunks of Ainne and Damaran are lost to this blighted zone.

**The Swars** – the marsh that spreads deep across the land, running from the west sea many miles inland in the southern portion of the crownlands. It is a dangerous place of black mana and missing souls, and those that prey and thrive on such things. Folklore has it, the marsh grows. If one were to consult hundreds of years old maps it would seem so, though scholars would say simply that old maps are inaccurate.
