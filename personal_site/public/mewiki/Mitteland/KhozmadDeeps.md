# Khozmad Deeps
The Deeps is the collective name for the near contiguous underground network stretching from under the Crownlands of Mitteland, beneath the Fold, and under the north-western most stretches of the Fold, with even some passages winding into Ihleiska and the Eastern Spur. Created in a manner of speaking at the same time the Fold was, linking and forming underground structures from Edenic ones that certainly existed before, but were almost entirely lost in the tumult.

Unlike most underground areas, where a few caverns and caves lead downwards and quickly (in relative terms) faded into the cosmological roots of the physical worlds, the Underdark, the Deeps are a vast and deep network of caves and caverns, rifts and holes stretching for over 800 miles from its furthest official west and east points. At first blush it is like the Underdark, with the sheer variety and size of its underground features, but thankfully unlike that place the orientation of down is only one, the connecting passages don’t fluctuate, and psychic weight is as normal for the Material World.

## The Dwarves during the Shattering
During the Shattering what underground communities did exist were decimated and all but entirely destroyed, and the dwarves, their holds and their cultural heritage were especially hard hit. With the death of so many dwarves and the loss of their homes, temples and history, the survivors fled to the surface. But the surface was neither welcoming, full as it was with other hard pressed and better suited survivors, nor fitting for them, and most tried to return to these newly formed caverns.

Over centuries clan honour demanded a Manifestation of Destiny, for the dwarves to reclaim the underneath. Year after year, they would head down, and tunnel by tunnel claim as much of, what was essentially new, territory “back”. The most famous of these campaigns, for its scale and its relatively recent history, was the “reclaiming” of the Braceholm Vast by Iman the Great in the name of the dwarves against Drow who had come to occupy the vast there, cementing him and his line as Emperor over the dwarves (despite being human). Dwarven loyalty was well earned, and they have been steadfast supporters of the ‘legitimate’ line ever since.

In order to hold this new underground, the much-diminished population also had to be recovered and was done so in the fashion of men and women, leading them ever further from their ancient origins and loosening whatever grip they had on the old ways. All this produced a unique dwarven culture unlike surface dwellers or those that came before, with Dwarven Bowers being formalised brothel institutions aimed at repopulated the vast underground with new dwarves even to this day (1801AC).

## The Vasts
Of the caverns of the deeps three are so vast they become like miniature worlds, and are the so called Vasts. Each is home to a city or three, and is a kingdom unto itself in a manner of speaking. All, now, nominally part of the Empire, of course.

The Vast of a Thousand Waters, **Torzen Vatners Vast**, channels most of the underground saltwater rivers tumbling down from the Wester Sea and the Swars. It is east near Avonsby, primarily placed to trade with the Heartlands through good connections to agrarian Avonsby and the highway that (at times) was the Lineé.

**The Vast of Ghostly Giants**, home to the world’s greatest known mushroom forest, with fungi reaching 150ft in height and over 30ft in girth. North of the Vabarian highlands and deep under the western portion of the Spur.

**The Braceholm Vast**, only recently brought back under the fold, named after the dwarven fortress city that dominates it, sitting under southern Vabaria and parts of the southern Fold.

**Ograhm’s Road** – the underground highway of caverns, caves and carved tunnels that link the Heartlands to the populated northern reaches of the Mid-South. Not a vast itself, its sheer length and string of settlements earns its place as a region unto itself. It is the simplest solution for accessing the Mid-South - for noone crosses the Fold lightly. Its the road, or a month-long journey perilosouly close to the Mists and through the dangerous elf-hunted Mountroot Forest otherwise.

## Tunnel Fighting in The Fold
For unfettered access to the nitre mines and veins of the southern Fold, claimed and patrolled by the dwarven clans of the Braceholm Vast that resolutely did not recognize the legitimacy of Stannis, the Tunnel Fights began. “As my ancestor cleared these tunnels of the Shadow’s blight, so will I of your stubbornness.” Only now it wasn’t to unseat Drow conquerors, but to conquer the dwarves themselves.

The four companies that went into the Fold faced the horrendous conditions and casualties of the Dwarven underground citadels, earned the cognomen “The Cantankerous”, and the single remaining company that was forged out of the survivors fought again against the Godsbridge Fortress, this time from two angles and without the same level of losses as before, becoming an elite company of the Army and one of Stannis personal guard.

The short war came to an end after taking the Grand Hall of Braceholm, and Stannis proclaiming his right on the monument of Drow dead, in the heart of those ancient halls of ancestors.
