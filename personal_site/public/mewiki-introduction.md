## Welcome

The MEWiki is an online repository for my worldbuilding project I call the 'Materium Existentiae'.

The goals of the project are:
- To provide easy access to setting information for players through articles and links to key terms
- To be thoroughly cross-linked (any mention of a person, place or fact will be automatically highlighted) via terms (which, where appropriate, link to articles)
- Any links can be hovered to open tooltips with core data about that term
- Tooltip term descriptions themselves will contain highlights, with tooltips able to appear on top of tooltips.
- To be thoroughly cross-navigable (any highlighted term that relates to a full page will have a link to that page in its tooltip)
- And for one day soon enable the addition of player-character-written articles and comments on main wiki pages, via a website form
